# Dhakkirni privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Dhakkirni does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no server of its own, and it makes no network requests of its own. Prayer times are calculated on your device.

## What stays on your device

- **Your location.** This is the latitude and longitude you type in, or the position Chrome gives the extension when you click "Use my location". It is used only to calculate prayer times. It is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on this device and is not synced to other devices.
- **Your settings**, such as the calculation method, per-prayer adjustments, reminder interval, quiet hours and language. They are kept in Chrome's sync storage, so if you use Chrome sync they are synced through your Google account like other Chrome settings. They contain no location.

You can delete all of it by removing the extension.

## What leaves your device

Nothing is sent by the extension. It has no host permissions and does not contact any website or server.

If you click "Use my location", Chrome itself may ask its location service to find your position. That request is made by the browser under Google's own policies, not by Dhakkirni, and the extension does not send the result anywhere.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
